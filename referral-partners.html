Frequently Asked Questions

How much do I earn per referral?

You earn £200-500 per successful enrollment, depending on the course value. Higher-value courses (like Master's degrees) earn higher commissions.

When do I get paid?

Payment is made within 30 days of the student's successful enrollment and course start date.

Is there a cost to become a partner?

No. It's completely free to join our referral partner network. No hidden fees or requirements.

How many students can I refer?

Unlimited. There's no cap on the number of referrals or your potential earnings.

What support do I get?

You'll receive marketing materials, referral links, email/SMS templates, and dedicated partner support from our team.

Do I need to handle the application process?

No. You simply make the introduction. We handle everything else - applications, Student Finance, interviews, and ongoing support.

What if my referral doesn't enroll?

No worries - you don't lose anything. You only earn commission when a student successfully enrolls and starts their course.
